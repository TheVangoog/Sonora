## What these numbers do and do not show

- They test the algorithm on clean sine tones. The error is far below ±5 cents because a sine's difference
  function is almost exactly a parabola near its minimum. A real guitar (harmonics, noise, the pitch
  falling slightly as the string decays) will give larger errors; that is not measured here.
- The built-in generator slider plays only whole hertz from 100 to 1000 Hz, so 83.37 Hz, 194.87 Hz and E2 cannot
  be set on the page; they were played by an OscillatorNode created from the console.
- The display adds smoothing (`pitchSmoother.ts`) and rounds cents to whole numbers.
